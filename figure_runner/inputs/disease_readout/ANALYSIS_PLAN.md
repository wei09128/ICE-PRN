# Disease-pathway annotation of chemical-response transfer

Locked 22 October 2026 before new gene-level outcomes. Exploratory development-data analysis.

Question: do destination-cancer-pathway genes occur more often among rescued or persistent-failure response genes than among both-success response genes?

Annotation: complete KEGG human Breast cancer hsa05224 and Prostate cancer hsa05215 gene lists retrieved 22 October 2026. Raw entries, complete extracted membership and SHA-256 hashes accompany this package. Map by exact Entrez gene ID to the frozen 978 measured L1000 landmarks. No inferred readouts, alias expansion, selective gene additions or outcome-guided list changes. Lists overlap and are broad pathway proxies, not exact MCF7/PC3 biology or exhaustive disease-associated genes. No distinction between an upstream regulator and a downstream readout is inferred from membership.

Inputs: exact previously exported response array, SHA-256 24e24107a57a60d487a8960c7e85f5b70c0a04aa8c77bb522736847342420b03. Original cohort, 4,902 IDs / 4,185 identity groups, RNA-plate halves, five identity folds, training weights, predictors and settings remain fixed. Original coefficients were not retained: replay the unchanged archived fit/predict functions for 20 fits. No tuning or new model selection. Before analysis, require all 100 direction/orientation/fold/method per-ID loss arrays to match the archived loss arrays within rtol 1e-8 and atol 1e-10. Parity failure blocks analysis.

Unit of classification: measured response gene, not drug or drug target. For each direction and technical orientation, average each gene's held-out squared error across IDs within chemical identity, then equally across identities. A method is better/worse only if its loss is strictly lower/higher than the destination-training mean in both orientations. Rescued = copy worse, cross-gene better; persistent failure = both worse; both success = both better; mixed/tied/other patterns stay separate. Own-target exclusion is not used for drugs.

Primary family: four coverage differences, two destination-disease/direction pairs x two contrasts (rescued minus both success; persistent failure minus both success). MCF7 to PC3 uses prostate membership; PC3 to MCF7 uses breast membership. Coverage denominator is all measured genes in that outcome group. Missing disease membership means outside these curated pathways, not proven absence of disease involvement. Report counts, fractions and differences whether positive, negative or unresolved.

Uncertainty: 5,000 multinomial resamples of chemical identities, seed 2026102207, identical draws across directions, methods, gene sets and technical orientations. Recompute gene classifications in every draw. Report conditional 95% intervals and approximate Bonferroni 98.75% marginal intervals across the four primary contrasts. Genes are not independent bootstrap units. If an observed or resampled comparison group is empty, report undefined draws and leave that interval unresolved; do not silently discard empty-group draws or enlarge lists. No independent-gene Fisher test or gene-label permutation is used. These intervals describe sampling of chemical responses conditional on fixed genes, annotations, fits and technical halves; they do not establish independent disease enrichment, causal mechanisms, or biological replication.

Secondary descriptive outputs: cross-disease coverage for each direction, ordinary conditional intervals, mixed-group coverage, and continuous copy/learned skills within each disease mask and its complement. These retain information if almost all genes are rescued. No secondary endpoint rescues a failed primary endpoint. Expression abundance, response size, shared cancer biology, correlated genes and technical reliability are unadjusted. Disease, lineage and cell-line differences cannot be separated here.

Outputs: gene outcomes and per-method losses; complete annotation coverage; all primary/secondary contrasts including undefined entries; source hashes; parity; conditional bootstrap draws; candidate figure. The full identity x gene loss cache remains local and is omitted from RETURN_TO_CHAT.zip. Preserve the original S2B and negative findings; no automatic manuscript replacement, candidate nomination, treatment ranking or AD-specific claim.
