# Rank by p53 readout sensitivity contract

23 October 2026. Exploratory follow-up on development-exposed K562/RPE1 predictions. Original whole-response rank curves and p53 target-annotation associations are known. This contract is fixed before examining new rank-specific p53 readout losses. It is not untouched confirmation or historical preregistration.

Question: as correction rank increases, does prediction improve preferentially within fixed external p53 target-gene readouts relative to other readouts? This links capacity to a readout annotation; it does not test whether a model has learned a p53 mechanism.

Readout annotation: the first table, Census p53 target genes by Fischer (2017), at https://tp53.cancer.gov/target_genes, captured 23 October 2026. Its 343 unique symbols are retained exactly; the NCI overview's approximate 350 count is not used to invent additional members. Exact legacy-symbol mapping yields 134 of the 7,226 measured readouts. The complement is the remaining 7,090 unambiguously mapped readouts; two HSPA14 coordinates remain excluded from pathway/complement evaluation. All 7,226 coordinates remain in protected all-readout scoring. No alias expansion, alternate p53 lists, target annotation substitution, response-driven member selection or program enlargement. This is distinct from the historical 102-readout conditional endpoint and the external A549 p53 stability-hit annotation of perturbed targets.

Replay unchanged original models on the exact original native split arrays and target-grouped folds. Retain ranks 0,1,2,4,8,16,32 from the common 40-column source basis; rank 0 is per-gene affine. The original 24-column rank-16 anchor is separate and descriptive, not substituted into the common-basis curve. Copy, zero and destination-mean comparators are retained. Source directions are ordered by source variance, not destination relevance. No rank selection, hyperparameter tuning, new model family or biological A inference.

Scoring: use held-out interventions and opposite destination technical halves. Exclude each perturbed target's own readout from every applicable mask. Average masked readout MSE within construct, then constructs within target, technical orientations and targets with equal target weights. Skill = 1 - model_MSE / destination_mean_MSE, using each readout set's own comparator loss. Never average target-level percentage skills.

Release gates: exact response archive SHA-256 checks; all 80 original anchor construct-loss checks; and all 44 saved direction/half/method whole-response target-loss vector comparisons from the original rank return, including the common-basis rank0-32 models. Any failed parity blocks biological interpretation. Freeze masks and export their hashes before running fits.

Primary family: 12 contrasts (six nonzero ranks x two directions), each [p53 skill at rank r - p53 affine skill] - [complement skill at rank r - complement affine skill]. Rank0's corresponding contrast is identically zero and descriptive. Original rank16 anchor is not a thirteenth primary test. Retain every rank and direction regardless of sign. Ordinary 95% intervals plus approximate Bonferroni-adjusted 99.583333% marginal intervals across all 12 contrasts. No selected early rank, saturation claim, direction interaction test or per-gene significance call.

Uncertainty: 10,000 shared target-ID cluster resamples, seed 2026102304, keeping methods, masks and directions together. Models, controls, masks, technical halves and annotation snapshots remain fixed; no refitting in the bootstrap. Invalid/zero denominators block endpoint intervals instead of omitting draws. Targets and readout genes are not independent biological replicates.

Interpretation: an early or late gain associates prediction capacity in this chosen source basis with p53 readouts; it does not locate p53 in an individual component, identify a pathway count, show rewiring, establish mediation, or distinguish p53 from correlated programs and measurement properties. Programs are unmatched for baseline abundance, repeatability and response scale. The source-variance ordering prevents interpreting high fitted rank as biological dimensionality. No main-figure replacement or manuscript claim is automatic. Candidate discovery, CARNIVAL, Jurkat confirmation and closed A/operator comparisons stay parked.
