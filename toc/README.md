# ICE-PRN TOC graphic

Rebuild: `python build_toc.py` (requires matplotlib).

Outputs: 3.25 × 1.75 inch vector PDF, editable-text SVG and 600-dpi PNG.

Genetic 10–12% summarizes original learned held-out skill (12.04% and 9.94%); chemical 3.2–3.6% summarizes 3.19% and 3.56%. Skill is MSE reduction against the destination training mean. Ranges summarize directions, not uncertainty intervals. The graphic does not compare transferability between platforms or identify a mechanism. The p53 association attenuated after matching on untreated baseline expression; balance and overlap were incomplete.

This is a separate proposed replacement for the previous TOC artwork. Manuscript files have not been changed.
